AutoEq data sources

autoeq_index.tsv lists headphone presets from the public jaakkopasanen/AutoEq
results repository (https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq). The app downloads
the selected preset from cdn.jsdelivr.net when the user picks it; see PRIVACY.md.

harman_in_ear_2019v2.csv and harman_over_ear_2018.csv are the AutoEq target
curves "Harman in-ear 2019" and "Harman over-ear 2018". The files are copied
unchanged from mpvRx (Riteshp2001/mpvRx).

AutoEq is MIT licensed; its license is in LICENSE-AutoEq.txt. Individual
measurements and brand names keep their own attribution and rights.

Harman curves are frequency-response references, not correction gains. The app
applies the selected headphone's downloaded preset, including its preamp.
